About me

Hey, I'm Finn Rietz, an AI researcher and computer scientist from Germany. I am currently finishing my Ph.D., which focuses on Deep Learning and Reinforcement Learning. While I spend most of my time building AI systems, I've recently become deeply interested in studying the societal and macroeconomic implications of AI. When I'm not training models or reading papers, I am usually out in the beautiful Swedish nature with our dog — who remains blissfully unaware of the everyday craziness in the AI world.



About this site

Primarily, this site serves as my personal space for discussing and presenting things I care about, or that I consider potentially useful or noteworthy for others. Additionally, this site also functions as an informal business card, hence a short version of my CV is available here.
